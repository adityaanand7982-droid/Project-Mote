// THE PROBLEM

◉ STATIONARY.SENSOR.PROBLEM
ERR-01

High Cost & Fixed Locations

Professional pollution meters cost thousands of dollars and are strictly stationary. They can only measure the exact spot they are bolted to.

ERR-02

Approximations vs. Reality

While you can check pollution levels on weather apps or Google/Apple Maps, that data is based on broad regional assumptions and approximations, not true ground-level measurements.I'm building a high-altitude data collection tool, not a smart thermostat..

[02]PROJECT-MOTE DEPLOYMENT

// THE SOLUTION

By attaching high-fidelity environmental sensors ( Bosch BME688 & BMV080) to a small drone, this payload completely changes how we capture and measure environmental data:

SOL-01

True Mobility

It is non-stationary. It can fly to cover massive distances, providing a much larger dynamic field of view for pollution tracking like you can see around your house witihin the drones range .

SOL-02

3D Data Collection

It measures real-time changes in microclimates across varying heights and altitudes, not just flat ground data which also helps to obserbe day to day change in polloution level of cities we live and our surroundings.

SOL-03

Extreme Accessibility

It gathers data in hazardous or hard-to-reach locations without requiring human intervention — like above dense forests, high mountains, heavily populated areas, or even active volcanoes to take measurements which further helps in many studies and sutaible control measures to be taken foregranted.

SOL-04

Cost-Effective

It replaces thousand-dollar stationary measuring instruments with a highly affordable, custom-engineered and made 3d printable pod which houses the sensors and the microcontroller. And to provide a much longer flight time for measurements the whole system gets powered by Li-ion cell not the typical Li-po battery pack.

SOL-05

Pinpoint Accuracy

It provides exact, real-time, daily measurements of the immediate surroundings rather than relying on generic map approximations which is used mainly by online maps.
